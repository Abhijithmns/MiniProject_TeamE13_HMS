Doctor Availability, Booking & Cancellation (FR-APPT-01, FR-APPT-02, FR-APPT-03)

        

          Demonstrates doctor working hours configuration with 15-minute slot intervals (FR-APPT-01), temporary slot locking with 5-minute checkout TTL (FR-APPT-02), atomic double-booking prevention under concurrent requests (TC-HMS-04), and automated booking confirmations (FR-APPT-04).

            Restricted to Doctors & Admins. Configures consultation working hours and automatically partitions time into 15-minute consultation slot intervals.

3. Double-Booking Prevention Test (TC-HMS-04 / Concurrency Simulation)

          

            Executes 2 simultaneous booking requests on the EXACT same slot at the same millisecond. Tests race condition isolation: Thread 1 succeeds with 201 Created; Thread 2 is atomically rejected with 409 Conflict ("Slot already booked").

4. Cancel or Reschedule an Appointment (US-08 / FR-APPT-03)

          

            Enforces the 2-hour policy (FR-APPT-03). Allows cancellation or change up to 2 hours before the scheduled time. Immediately releases the slot back to the public pool and updates patient history status to "Cancelled" or "Rescheduled".
